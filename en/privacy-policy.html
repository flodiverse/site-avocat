Privacy Policy

This notice explains, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), what personal data we collect through this website, why we use it, who receives it and what your rights are.

Version: v1 — 26 September 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is the Irina-Maria Peter Law Office, attorney at law and member of the Bucharest Bar, with its registered office in Sector 2, Bucharest, Romania.

For any question about the processing of your data you can reach us by email at av.irinapeter@gmail.com or by phone at +40 785 117 334.

2. What data we collect

Through the contact form on the Contact page we collect the following data, which you provide directly:

  • your full name;
  • your email address;
  • your phone number (optional);
  • the area of law you are interested in;
  • the description of your situation, as you choose to write it;
  • the date and time of submission, together with the version of this policy you accepted when submitting.

If you contact us directly by phone, email or WhatsApp, we process the data you share in the course of that communication.

When you visit the website, the hosting provider and the web font provider automatically process technical data (IP address, browser type, page requested), as detailed in section 5.

3. Purpose and legal basis of processing

We process your data for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

  • Responding to your request and arranging a consultation — legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, as the processing is necessary in order to take steps at your request prior to entering into a legal assistance contract.
  • Complying with the legal obligations of the legal profession (professional records, tax and archiving duties) — legal basis: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Website security, spam prevention and the defence of the office's rights — legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest.

Ticking the box “I have read and agree to the Privacy Policy” confirms that you have read this notice. Providing the fields marked as required in the form is necessary for us to reply; without them we cannot act on your request.

4. Special categories of data

Describing a legal situation may involve sensitive data: health, ethnic origin or religion, sexual orientation, criminal convictions or pending criminal proceedings. We recommend that you do not include such details in the form and that you keep them for the consultation itself, which takes place under the protection of legal professional privilege.

If you nevertheless choose to include such information, by submitting the form and ticking the agreement box you give your explicit consent to its processing for the purpose of assessing your request (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw this consent at any time by email, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Recipients of the data and international transfers

We do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing purposes. The data is accessed only by the attorney and, to the extent strictly necessary, by the following technical service providers:

  • Web3Forms (Web3Creative, India) — the service that receives the content of the form and forwards it to the office's email address, acting as a processor. According to its privacy policy, Web3Forms keeps a copy of the submission for no longer than 3 years from submission, after which it is deleted automatically; the office may request earlier deletion. The provider uses infrastructure in the United States (Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, Hetzner) and anti-spam services (CleanTalk, Akismet). Transfers of data to India and the USA rely on the standard contractual clauses provided for in Article 46(2)(c) GDPR, incorporated in the data processing agreement concluded with the provider.
  • Google LLC (USA) — the provider of the email service through which we receive and keep correspondence, and of the web fonts displayed on the site (Google Fonts). When pages load, your browser sends your IP address to Google's servers to deliver the fonts. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) — the website hosting provider (GitHub Pages), which processes technical access logs (IP address, time, page) in order to deliver and secure the service. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) — only if you choose to contact us through the WhatsApp button on the site. That communication is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Data may be disclosed to public authorities where the law requires it, subject to the attorney's duty of professional secrecy.

6. Retention period

  • Requests not followed by a legal assistance contract: the data is kept for no longer than 3 years from the last communication, in line with the general limitation period, and is then deleted.
  • Requests followed by a contract: the data becomes part of the client file and is kept for the period required by the legislation governing the legal profession and by tax and archiving obligations.
  • Copy of the submission held by Web3Forms: no longer than 3 years from submission, according to the provider's policy, or less at the office's request.
  • Technical logs of the hosting provider: according to its retention policy, usually no more than a few weeks.

7. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • the right of access to the data we hold about you (Article 15);
  • the right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data (Article 16);
  • the right to erasure, within the limits of the law (Article 17);
  • the right to restriction of processing (Article 18);
  • the right to data portability for the data you provided (Article 20);
  • the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Article 21);
  • the right to withdraw consent, where processing relies on it, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing (Article 7(3));
  • the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), 28-30 G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru Blvd., Sector 1, Bucharest, www.dataprotection.ro, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence, and to seek a judicial remedy (Articles 77-79).

8. How to exercise your rights

To exercise any of your rights, send a request to av.irinapeter@gmail.com. We will reply within one month of receiving your request; in complex cases this period may be extended by two months, in which case you will be informed. To protect your data, we may ask for additional information to verify your identity.

9. Data security and professional secrecy

The website is served exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the form is submitted over the same secure channel. Access to incoming correspondence is restricted to the attorney. Beyond the GDPR, all information you entrust to us is protected by the attorney's duty of professional secrecy under Romanian Law no. 51/1995 on the organisation and practice of the legal profession and the Statute of the profession.

10. Cookies

This website does not use cookies and does not use traffic analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking tools. The only technical data processed when you visit the site is described in section 5 and is needed to deliver the pages and fonts.

11. Minors

Our services are addressed to persons with full legal capacity. We do not knowingly collect data from persons under 16; where a minor needs legal assistance, the request must be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in legislation or in the services we use. The version in force is always published on this page, with the version number and date shown below. The version you accepted when submitting a form is recorded together with your message.
